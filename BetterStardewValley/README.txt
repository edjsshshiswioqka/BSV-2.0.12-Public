Better Stardew Valley (BSV) 2.0.14 beta
=======================================

Requirements: Stardew Valley 1.6.15 and SMAPI 4.5.2 or newer.
Optional: Generic Mod Config Menu (in-game settings), Automate.

Install
1. Quit the game.
2. Put the "BetterStardewValley" folder into Stardew Valley/Mods.
3. Start the game through SMAPI.

Upgrading from BSV 1.0.x or an earlier 2.0 build
1. Quit the game and back up your save folder.
2. Delete the old Mods/BetterStardewValley folder, then copy in the new one.
   You may keep your old config.json, fishing-machine.json and crab-catcher.json.
3. Existing saves continue: machines keep their level, progress and running cycle, the greenhouse grows to
   35 x 20 and items keep their IDs. If you never changed the 1.0 machine settings, the machines move to the
   2.0 defaults (2/3/4 days per cycle, 200/300/400 bait).

Multiplayer: every player needs the same BSV version.

Compatibility: please remove "All Professions" (BSV has its own, with a switch per skill in the settings) and
"Bigger Backpack" (BSV already expands the backpack). SpaceCore skills such as YACS cooking are supported.

Uninstalling: not recommended in the middle of a save. BSV items, machines and the expanded greenhouse and
cellar, the greenhouse upgrades and the Two-Story Shed depend on the mod.
Items in backpack slots 37-60 are hidden (not deleted) while BSV is not installed.

This is a beta. Please report problems on the Nexus page (mod 53251) with your SMAPI log (https://smapi.io/log).

Better Stardew Valley（BSV）2.0.14 测试版

需要：星露谷物语 1.6.15，SMAPI 4.5.2 或更新版本。
可选：Generic Mod Config Menu（游戏内设置菜单）、Automate。

安装
1. 退出游戏。
2. 把 "BetterStardewValley" 文件夹放进 Stardew Valley/Mods。
3. 通过 SMAPI 启动游戏。

从 BSV 1.0.x 或更早的 2.0 版本升级
1. 退出游戏，先备份存档文件夹。
2. 删掉旧的 Mods/BetterStardewValley 文件夹，再放入新的。
   旧的 config.json、fishing-machine.json、crab-catcher.json 可以保留。
3. 原来的存档可以继续玩：机器保留等级、进度和正在进行的那一轮，温室自动扩到 35×20，物品编号不变。
   如果你从没改过 1.0 的机器设置，机器会换成 2.0 的默认值（每轮 2/3/4 天，鱼饵 200/300/400）。

联机：所有玩家要装同一版本的 BSV。

兼容性：请删除 "All Professions"（BSV 自带全职业系统，设置里每个技能都有开关）和 "Bigger Backpack"
（BSV 已经扩大了背包）。支持 SpaceCore 技能，例如 YACS 烹饪技能。

卸载：不建议在存档中途卸载。BSV 的物品、机器、扩建的温室和地窖、温室升级以及双层棚屋都依赖本 mod。
卸载期间，背包第 37-60 格的物品会被隐藏（不会删除）。

这是测试版。遇到问题请到 N 网页面（mod 53251）反馈，并附上 SMAPI 日志（https://smapi.io/log）。
